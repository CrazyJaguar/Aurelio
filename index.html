Our Story

A love letter to coffee, community, and Kathmandu.

☕
How it began

From a small cart to a landmark

Aurelio started as a modest coffee cart at Thamel's morning market in 2013. Our founder, Sushant Raj Mishra, had just returned from three years learning the craft in Milan and Melbourne. She brought back more than recipes — she brought a philosophy.

Within two years, the cart became a kiosk. The kiosk became a café. Today, Aurelio is Kathmandu's most-loved specialty coffee destination, serving over 400 guests daily across two floors of our flagship Thamel location.

What hasn't changed: every bean is still hand-selected, every drink still made with the same care Sushant put into that very first cup.

Our Values

What we stand for

🌱
Sustainability First
Compostable packaging, direct trade sourcing, solar water heating, and zero-waste kitchen practices.
🤝
Community Driven
Monthly open mic nights, local artist showcases, and partnerships with Kathmandu's creative community.
🎓
Education & Craft
Free monthly cupping sessions, barista workshops, and our annual Coffee Origins tasting event.
